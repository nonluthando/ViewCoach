# Screening Interviews

A screening interview is an early-stage conversation used to confirm basic fit
before a longer technical or final-round process. It commonly lasts 15 to 45
minutes.

## Recruiter screening

A recruiter screen usually checks role interest, eligibility, location,
availability, salary expectations, communication, and whether the candidate's
experience broadly matches the vacancy. Prepare a concise introduction, a clear
reason for applying, and one or two relevant project examples.

## Technical screening

A technical screen checks whether the candidate can explain core concepts and
apply them at the expected level. Junior candidates are normally evaluated on
fundamentals, reasoning, communication, and learning ability rather than senior
architecture depth.

## Preparation

Read the job description closely. Match each important requirement to coursework,
projects, work experience, or a learning plan. Practise explaining decisions and
trade-offs without memorising a script word for word.
