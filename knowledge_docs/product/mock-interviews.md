# Mock Interviews

Mock interviews provide timed practice using questions already available in
ViewCoach.

## Formats

Sessions can focus on technical, conceptual, behavioural, debugging or
mixed interview preparation.

## Question selection

Question selection can favour due questions, recent difficulty and material
relevant to the selected goal or focus.

## During a session

Users can work through prompts, record response notes and assess confidence.
A session should preserve progress and support pause and resume behaviour
where appropriate.

## Debrief

Completed sessions should show what was attempted, confidence, difficult
areas and useful next actions. The debrief feeds future preparation instead
of existing only as a historical score.
