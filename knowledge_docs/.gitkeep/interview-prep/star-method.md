# STAR Method

STAR is a structure for answering behavioural interview questions with a real
example.

## Situation

Give only the context needed to understand the example. State the project,
workplace, team, or problem without spending most of the answer on background.

## Task

Explain what you were responsible for or what outcome you needed to achieve.
Separate your responsibility from the wider team's responsibility.

## Action

Describe the steps you personally took. Include important decisions, collaboration,
tools, and how you responded when something changed or failed.

## Result

Explain the outcome and what changed because of your actions. Use a number when a
genuine metric exists, but do not invent one. End with a lesson or what you would
improve when that reflection is relevant.

## Screening length

For an initial interview, a STAR answer is usually strongest when it is focused and
easy to follow. Aim to make the action section the most detailed part.
