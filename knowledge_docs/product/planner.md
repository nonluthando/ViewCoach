# Daily Planner

The ViewCoach daily planner turns available study time into a focused
preparation plan. It considers due reviews, active roadmap progress,
recent weak areas, interview goals and suitable practice questions.

## Available time

Enter the amount of focused study time available for the day. ViewCoach
uses this as a planning budget rather than as a forced stopping point.

The generated recommendations should use the available time as closely as
practical. Any time that cannot be assigned should be shown as unallocated
instead of silently disappearing.

## Review, Learn and Practice

Daily work is grouped into three coherent sections.

Review contains due or overdue spaced-review questions. Learn contains
focused roadmap work. Practice contains weak-area recovery, fresh
interview questions and question-preparation work.

## Planning priorities

Due reviews are time-sensitive and normally receive priority. Active and
incomplete roadmap topics provide the main learning blocks. Recent Again
or Hard results can create weak-area work. Fresh practice is used to
balance the session when time remains.

## Focused roadmap blocks

Roadmap learning should use meaningful focused blocks rather than many
shallow topic switches. A roadmap block normally begins at 45 minutes and
may be deepened when the plan has additional time.

## Why a task was selected

Each recommendation includes an explanation based on signals such as due
review urgency, goal relevance, deadline proximity, recent difficulty and
continuity with work already in progress.

## Session timing

A study session tracks focused time separately from breaks. Users can pause
and resume, add 15 or 30 minutes, continue after reaching the planned
budget, or end deliberately. ViewCoach stores planned and actual focused
minutes for the session.
