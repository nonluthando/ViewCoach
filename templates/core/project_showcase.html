Problem

Product demo

A complete seeded product journey—not an empty account.

Architecture

Modular Django domains joined through services, not template logic.

Application layer

Accounts, goals, roadmaps, questions, reviews, planner, evidence, interviews and knowledge.

Persistence

PostgreSQL relational integrity, transactional workflows and pgvector retrieval.

Planning

Candidate generation, explainable scoring, compulsory work and OR-Tools optimisation.

AI boundary

Grounded assistance and editable drafts that remain outside review until approved.

Engineering decisions

The trade-offs are part of the product story.

One roadmap domain, several source types

Curated, imported and personal paths reuse progress and planning while retaining ownership.

Trade-off: stricter source-aware routing for far less duplicated behaviour.

Human approval before AI content enters review

Generated cards remain editable drafts until the candidate confirms them.

Trade-off: one extra step for better trust and retrieval quality.

Optimisation with a deterministic fallback

The planner can optimise within a time budget but still works when the solver is unavailable.

Trade-off: two selection paths that require shared policy tests.

Isolated demo workspaces

Visitors receive temporary accounts instead of sharing mutable credentials.

Trade-off: cleanup and capacity controls for a reliable portfolio experience.
