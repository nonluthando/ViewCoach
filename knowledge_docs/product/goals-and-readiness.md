# Interview Goals and Readiness

Interview goals connect preparation work to a role, opportunity, assessment
or upcoming interview.

## Primary goal

A user can maintain multiple active goals and mark one as primary. The
primary goal can influence roadmap ordering, planner scoring and practice
relevance.

## Interview stages

A goal may contain stages such as application, online assessment,
technical interview, behavioural interview or final interview. Stages can
include dates and preparation deadlines.

## Readiness

Readiness should use several explainable signals rather than a single
activity count. Relevant signals include roadmap coverage, prepared
questions, review consistency, mock-interview completion, confidence and
personal evidence readiness.

## Deadlines

Near deadlines can increase the priority of relevant preparation work, but
due reviews and required learning prerequisites may still override lower
value tasks.
