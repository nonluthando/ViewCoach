# Personal Evidence and Behavioural Stories

Personal evidence connects interview preparation to work the user has
actually completed.

## Evidence sources

Evidence may come from projects, employment, coursework, leadership,
teamwork, technical incidents and important decisions.

## Connections

Evidence can be linked to roadmap topics, interview questions, behavioural
stories and interview goals. These links help users explain where they
applied a concept rather than only defining it.

## Behavioural stories

The Story Bank structures reusable examples around the situation, task,
action, result, decisions, trade-offs and lessons learned.

## Privacy

Personal evidence belongs to the user. Global product documentation and
private user context must remain separate in retrieval and access control.
The first Help Assistant milestone retrieves only trusted global
documentation.
